# Attribution and reuse

## Original data

The worldwide metadata, resistance-marker table and README are provided by MalariaGEN through the [Pf8 open dataset](https://www.malariagen.net/data_package/open-dataset-plasmodium-falciparum-v80/). Retain their source attribution and the applicable [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The original files have been copied without editing; source URLs and hashes are recorded in `07_Reproducibility/source_manifest.json`.

The Kilifi CSVs and example outputs were generated from those files by the supplied scripts. They are derived resources, not new sample collection or new sequencing. Cite the Pf8 publication and identify the source release, selection rules and the group's analytical changes.

Suggested data acknowledgement: “This project uses public MalariaGEN Pf8 data contributed by its partner studies. We analysed sequence-derived pfcrt codon-76 calls from the specified Kilifi subset. Original data processing and sample collection were performed by the data producers.” Adapt the wording to the final analysis and follow the provider's citation guidance.

- Wamae et al. (2019), coastal Kenyan longitudinal analysis; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1128/AAC.01067-19). PDF obtained from the [University of Glasgow repository](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/325341/).

Fidock et al. is provided as citation and online links. Other external sites retain their own terms. The bibliography is a starting aid; check article versions and the citation format your lecturer requires.
