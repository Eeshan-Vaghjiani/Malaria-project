## Reading the summary tables

`n_qc` counts retained QC-passing records; `n_callable` is the primary denominator. `n_unambiguous` is the secondary denominator. `n_K_only`, `n_T_only`, `n_mixed_KT` and `n_uncallable` are category counts. `sampling_status` distinguishes observed years from years without samples.

The prefixes `carrier`, `unambiguous`, `K_only`, `T_only` and `mixed_KT` each have `_proportion`, `_ci_low` and `_ci_high` columns. All are on a 0–1 scale. The category intervals are individual binomial intervals, not simultaneous confidence regions for the full composition. Blank proportions for absent years must remain missing.

In the sensitivity table, `pooled_carrier` weights years by their callable sample counts. `equal_year_carrier` gives each observed year in a period equal weight. The latter is a different descriptive statistic; it does not make the archive population-representative. `observed_years` excludes absent years. The largest study happens to cover every observed year in this snapshot, but its sample sizes are also uneven.

## Questions for the group

- Does the broad historical pattern agree under both outcome definitions? Where does the difference matter most?
- Which years have the least precise estimates, and how does their sample size explain this?
- How do pooled and equally weighted summaries differ? Is the difference scientifically important?
- Does restricting to the largest contributing study change the interpretation? What residual sampling differences remain?
- How do your conclusions compare with Omedo et al. and Wamae et al., allowing for different samples, assays and definitions?

The chi-square test asks whether carriage and the chosen periods are associated in these archived samples. It does not test a monotonic trend, isolate a policy effect or measure treatment efficacy. It assumes independent observations; recorded case linkage was checked, but archive design may still limit inference. Read the [SciPy method documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.chi2_contingency.html) before interpreting it.

A year with no detected 76T still has uncertainty: use its interval and denominator. It is not proof that the marker was absent throughout Kilifi. The data end in 2020 and cannot establish the current prevalence or justify a change in treatment.
