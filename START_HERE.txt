VERIFIED DATA SNAPSHOT
The original metadata contains 33,325 records worldwide. The accompanying
marker table contains 24,409 QC-passing records worldwide. Filtering to
Kenya / Kilifi / 1994–2020 gives 2,078 metadata records; 206 fail source QC.
The retained dataset has 1,872 samples: 1,236 K-only, 467 T-only and 169
mixed K/T calls at codon 76. All retained samples have callable codon-76 data.
The unambiguous subset has 1,703 records. There are no retained samples for
2001 or 2002. Those years are missing observations, not zero prevalence.

WHAT YOU ARE ACTUALLY MEASURING
Primary: the proportion of callable samples in which 76T is detected,
including mixed K/T calls. Secondary: T-only among unambiguous K-only or
T-only samples. These are different sample-level measures. Neither is
the fraction of all parasite clones carrying the mutation.
